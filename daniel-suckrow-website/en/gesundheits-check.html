Your details

Your health is more than a number.

Your health and your worth as a person cannot be reduced to a number. The health scale only summarises part of your answers as rough guidance; it is not a diagnosis. A lower score can be a reason to calmly look at what might do you good. The result is not meant to create pressure or stress. If you have or suspect an eating disorder, please do not use this health check.

Questions and answers

What does the score from 0 to 100 mean?

Eight criteria give 0 or 10 points each. For alcohol and cigarettes, quantity-dependent deductions are subtracted from 10 starting points each. The score is capped at 0. The point thresholds say nothing about amounts that are safe for health. The score is editorial and not medically validated. An optional VO₂max fitness bonus adds up to 30 extra points; a maximum of 130 total points is possible. The total is not a percentage.

Do I need to complete every field?

Only age and eligibility context are required. Individual calculators need additional inputs. The habits score appears only when its criteria have complete inputs. Your entries are not transmitted to a server.
