HOW IT WORKS01 — 07

Steps marked in green are only included in the Complete package.

01

Initial 30-minute introductory call (Complete package only)

If you have questions before booking, you can book a free, no-obligation online introductory call of about 15 minutes. It is optional and not part of the booked consultation package. The Complete package still includes a personal video introduction of about 30 minutes. In the Compact package, documents to fill in replace the introduction.

02

One week of food tracking

You document your meals and drinks for a week without artificially changing your usual diet. I'll provide you with the document for recording it.

03

Medical blood test

I recommend that you have a blood test done by your family doctor during the same week. The organization and possible costs of the examination are up to you.

04

Individual evaluation

I look at your nutritional protocol and the medical blood values you provide in context.

05

Personalized PDF

You will receive an individual PDF with understandable results and concrete, everyday recommendations - tailored to your preferences and routines.

06

Personal review call · about 30 minutes (Complete package only)

The Complete package includes a final online call of about 30 minutes in which we discuss your evaluation and open questions. In the Compact package, we handle questions by email.

07

Contact if you have any further questions

Even after the consultation, you can contact me by email if you have further questions. info@veganiel.com
