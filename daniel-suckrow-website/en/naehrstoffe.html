All nutrients

BS

Fibre

Fibre keeps you full, feeds gut bacteria and keeps blood sugar steady. Most people eat too little of it.

Animal productsLearn more ↗Ca

Calcium

Calcium is the main building block of bones and teeth. Without dairy, it pays to keep an eye on it.

Plant-basedLearn more ↗Fe

Iron

Iron carries oxygen in the blood. Plant iron is absorbed less well, but absorption is easy to improve.

Plant-basedLearn more ↗Ω3

EPA and DHA (omega-3)

EPA and DHA are the long-chain omega-3 fatty acids that the heart, brain and eyes particularly need. They are found in oily sea fish and algae oil.

Plant-basedAnimal productsLearn more ↗B9

Folate

Folate is important for cell division and blood formation. Green vegetables and pulses are excellent sources.

Animal productsLearn more ↗SFA

Saturated fat

Saturated fat is mainly found in animal foods as well as coconut and palm fat. Too much of it raises LDL cholesterol.

Animal productsLearn more ↗I

Iodine

The thyroid needs iodine for its hormones. Germany is an iodine-deficient region, which is why iodised salt matters.

Plant-basedAnimal productsLearn more ↗Mg

Magnesium

Magnesium is involved in muscle and nerve function. Plant-based diets usually provide plenty.

Learn more ↗P

Protein

Protein supplies amino acids for muscles, enzymes, hormones and the immune system.

Learn more ↗NaCl

Salt

Most people eat far too much salt – mainly from bread, sausage, cheese and ready meals.

Plant-basedAnimal productsLearn more ↗SPS

Phytochemicals

Polyphenols, carotenoids, glucosinolates and many more: thousands of compounds that only plants make. They are considered protective for the heart, blood vessels and cells.

Animal productsLearn more ↗Se

Selenium

Selenium protects cells and supports the thyroid. European soils are low in selenium, so plants here often contain little.

Plant-basedLearn more ↗B12

Vitamin B12

Vitamin B12 is made only by microorganisms. With a fully plant-based diet, a supplement is essential.

Plant-basedLearn more ↗D

Vitamin D

The body mainly makes vitamin D with sunlight. From October to March, the sun in Germany is not strong enough.

Plant-basedAnimal productsLearn more ↗Zn

Zinc

Zinc is part of over 300 enzymes. Whole grains and pulses provide plenty, but also contain phytate, which inhibits absorption.
