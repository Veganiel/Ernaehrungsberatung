Nutrient guide

Folate

Folate is important for cell division and blood formation. Green vegetables and pulses are excellent sources.

Keep an eye on it when eating animal products
B9

What the body needs it for

  • Cell division and growth
  • Blood formation
  • Especially important before and during pregnancy

How much you need

Adults: 300 µg folate equivalents per day (DGE). When trying to conceive, an additional 400 µg folic acid.

Good plant sources

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Chickpeas, dryapprox. 340 µg
  • Edamameapprox. 300 µg
  • Lentils, dryapprox. 180 µg
  • Kaleapprox. 190 µg
  • Spinachapprox. 145 µg
  • Broccoliapprox. 110 µg

How to make the most of it

  • Cook vegetables gently: folate is sensitive to heat.
  • Plan something green and some pulses every day.
  • Freshly prepared food contains more folate than food kept warm for long.
