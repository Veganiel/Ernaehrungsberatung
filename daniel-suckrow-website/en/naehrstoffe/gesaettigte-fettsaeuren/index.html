Nutrient guide

Saturated fat

Saturated fat is mainly found in animal foods as well as coconut and palm fat. Too much of it raises LDL cholesterol.

Keep an eye on it when eating animal products
SFA

What the body needs it for

  • Provide energy
  • Are part of cell membranes
  • Unfavourable for blood lipids and heart in larger amounts

How much you need

At most 10 % of energy, so below about 22 g per day at 2,000 kcal. Better replaced with unsaturated fats from nuts, seeds and plant oils.

Where a lot of it is found

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Coconut fatapprox. 87 g
  • Butterapprox. 51 g
  • Palm fatapprox. 50 g
  • Whipping cream (30 %)approx. 19 g
  • Hard cheeseapprox. 20 g
  • Salamiapprox. 15 g

How to make the most of it

  • Eat sausage, butter, cheese and fatty meat less often.
  • Use olive oil or cold-pressed rapeseed oil instead of butter.
  • Swap meat for pulses and tofu now and then.
