Nutrient guide

Iodine

The thyroid needs iodine for its hormones. Germany is an iodine-deficient region, which is why iodised salt matters.

Keep an eye on it with a plant-based dietKeep an eye on it when eating animal products
I

What the body needs it for

  • Production of thyroid hormones
  • Metabolism and energy balance
  • Development in pregnancy and childhood

How much you need

Adults: about 200 µg per day, from 51 years 180 µg (DGE).

Good plant sources

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Iodised salt (per 1 g)approx. 2 µg
  • Nori seaweed (highly variable)approx. 1,500 µg

How to make the most of it

  • Always use iodised salt at home instead of sea salt or Himalayan salt.
  • Avoid kombu and hijiki: they can contain extremely high amounts of iodine.
  • With a fully plant-based diet, a supplement often makes sense – ideally discuss this individually.
