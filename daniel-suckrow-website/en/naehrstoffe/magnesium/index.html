Nutrient guide

Magnesium

Magnesium is involved in muscle and nerve function. Plant-based diets usually provide plenty.

Mg

What the body needs it for

  • Muscle and nerve function
  • Energy metabolism
  • Bones

How much you need

Adults: men 350 mg, women 300 mg per day (DGE estimate).

Good plant sources

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Hulled hemp seedsapprox. 700 mg
  • Pumpkin seedsapprox. 550 mg
  • Cashewsapprox. 270 mg
  • Dark chocolate 70 %approx. 230 mg
  • Quinoaapprox. 200 mg
  • Oatsapprox. 140 mg

How to make the most of it

  • A handful of seeds or nuts a day already covers a lot.
  • Whole grains provide far more than white flour.
  • Magnesium-rich mineral water counts too.
