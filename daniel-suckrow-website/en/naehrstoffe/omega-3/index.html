EPA and DHA (omega-3)

EPA and DHA are the long-chain omega-3 fatty acids that the heart, brain and eyes particularly need. They are found in oily sea fish and algae oil.

Keep an eye on it with a plant-based dietKeep an eye on it when eating animal products
Ω3

What the body needs it for

  • Heart and blood vessels
  • Brain and eyes, especially DHA
  • Keeping inflammatory processes in balance

How much you need

For EPA and DHA combined, EFSA gives 250 mg per day. The body can convert the plant precursor ALA into EPA and DHA only to a small extent.

Where EPA and DHA are found

  • Algae oil – the direct plant source of EPA and DHA
  • Oily sea fish such as herring, mackerel or salmon
  • Precursor ALA: linseed oil, flaxseed, chia seeds, walnuts, hemp seeds

How to make the most of it

  • With a fully plant-based diet, an algae oil supplement makes sense.
  • If you eat fish less than 2 to 3 times a week, you should also keep an eye on EPA and DHA.
  • Plan ALA sources such as linseed oil, flaxseed or walnuts every day.
