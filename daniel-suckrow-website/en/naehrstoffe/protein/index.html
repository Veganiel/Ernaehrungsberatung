Nutrient guide

Protein

Protein supplies amino acids for muscles, enzymes, hormones and the immune system.

P

What the body needs it for

  • Builds and maintains muscles, skin and organs
  • Is needed for enzymes and hormones
  • Supports the immune system

How much you need

Adults: 0.8 g per kg of body weight, from 65 years 1.0 g (DGE). Active people often need more.

Good plant sources

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Hulled hemp seedsapprox. 30 g
  • Pumpkin seedsapprox. 30 g
  • Seitanapprox. 25 g
  • Lentils, dryapprox. 24 g
  • Tempehapprox. 19 g
  • Tofuapprox. 14 g
  • Oatsapprox. 13 g

How to make the most of it

  • Spread protein over several meals.
  • Combine pulses and grains over the day.
  • Calculate your needs with the protein calculator.
