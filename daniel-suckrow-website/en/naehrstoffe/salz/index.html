Nutrient guide

Salt

Most people eat far too much salt – mainly from bread, sausage, cheese and ready meals.

Keep an eye on it with a plant-based dietKeep an eye on it when eating animal products
NaCl

What the body needs it for

  • Sodium regulates fluid balance
  • Important for nerves and muscles
  • Too much can raise blood pressure

How much you need

At most 6 g of salt per day (DGE). Average intake in Germany is considerably higher.

Where a lot of it is found

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Soy sauceapprox. 14 g
  • Salamiapprox. 4.5 g
  • Cooked hamapprox. 2.5 g
  • Hard cheeseapprox. 2 g
  • Crispsapprox. 1.5 g
  • Breadapprox. 1.2 g

How to make the most of it

  • Use iodised salt at home, but sparingly.
  • Season with herbs, spices, lemon and garlic.
  • Cut down on sausage, cheese and ready meals – that is where most salt hides.
