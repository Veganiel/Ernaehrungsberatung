Nutrient guide

Zinc

Zinc is part of over 300 enzymes. Whole grains and pulses provide plenty, but also contain phytate, which inhibits absorption.

Keep an eye on it with a plant-based diet
Zn

What the body needs it for

  • Immune system and wound healing
  • Skin, hair and nails
  • Cell division and metabolism

How much you need

Depending on phytate intake according to the DGE: men 11 to 16 mg, women 7 to 10 mg per day. With a plant-based diet, the upper value applies.

Good plant sources

Content per 100 g, rounded. Values vary by variety and product.

  • Hulled hemp seedsapprox. 10 mg
  • Pumpkin seedsapprox. 7 mg
  • Cashewsapprox. 5.8 mg
  • Oatsapprox. 4 mg
  • Lentils, dryapprox. 3.5 mg
  • Tofuapprox. 1.5 mg

How to make the most of it

  • Sourdough bread and soaked pulses improve absorption.
  • Plan nuts and seeds into every day.
  • Sprouts and tempeh contain less phytate.
