What is important to me

Nutrition should fit with life.

You will receive a structured inventory, an understandable classification and a concrete guide for your next steps.

i.

Your own path.

Your habits, preferences and goals are our starting point. Together we find steps that work in your daily life.

ii.

Knowledge you can use.

I value clear explanations of scientific findings and a thoughtful approach to health information.

iii.

Plant-focused.

Plant variety, sustainability and animal welfare matter to me, and I’m happy to share ideas. What suits you is entirely your decision.

How much does the consultation cost?

There are two packages: Complete for a one-off €79 and Compact for a one-off €49. There is no membership and no subscription.

Do I have to be or become vegan?

No. You don’t have to be vegan or vegetarian – and I won’t try to talk you into it. Plant-focused eating is my personal passion, and I’m happy to show you where plant foods can enrich your everyday life. What suits you is your decision. We always start from your habits, your preferences and your everyday life.

What is the difference between Complete and Compact?

Both have the same content: food tracking, individual evaluation and a personalised PDF. Complete also includes a video introduction and a video review call, each about 30 minutes. With Compact, documents to fill in replace the introduction, and we handle questions by email.

Is the blood test included in the price?

No. You have the medical blood test done by your family doctor – ideally in the same week as your food tracking. Arranging the test and any costs are your responsibility.

Do I have to book an introductory call first?

No. The free introductory call of about 15 minutes is optional and not part of the consultation package. The Complete package still includes a personal video introduction of about 30 minutes. In the Compact package, documents to fill in replace the introduction.

Do I need to change my diet during the tracking week?

No. For one week, you record your meals and drinks without artificially changing your usual diet.

How does booking work?

You pay once via PayPal. After confirmed payment, you will receive an email confirmation. With Complete, we then arrange your appointment by email; with Compact, I send you the documents by email.

Do the sessions take place online?

In the Complete package, the introduction and the review call take place online via video. The Compact package works without calls – we handle questions by email.

Does the consultation replace a visit to the doctor?

No. The nutrition consultation and the interpretation of the findings you provide do not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Medical decisions and the interpretation of abnormal findings belong in the hands of a doctor.

What happens after the review call?

Even after the consultation, you can contact me by email if you have further questions.
