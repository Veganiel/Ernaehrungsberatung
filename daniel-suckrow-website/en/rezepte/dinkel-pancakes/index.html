Breakfast

Wholegrain spelt pancakes with berries

Fluffy pancakes made with wholegrain spelt flour, sweetened only with banana, served with soy yoghurt, berries and walnuts.

  • Prep10 min
  • Total25 min
  • Servings2
  • Protein per servingapprox. 17 g

Ingredients

serves 2

  • 120 g wholegrain spelt flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 200 ml soy drink
  • 1 tsp cold-pressed rapeseed oil for frying
  • 150 g plain soy yoghurt
  • 150 g berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tbsp chopped walnuts

Method

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, salt and flaxseed in a bowl.
  2. Mash the banana finely with a fork and stir in the soy drink. Stir into the flour mixture and leave the batter to rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Heat a pan with a little oil over medium heat. Add 2 tbsp batter per pancake and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Serve with soy yoghurt, berries and walnuts.

The protein figure is a rough estimate based on average values for the ingredients. To find out how much protein you need per day, use the Protein calculator.

Tips

  • The banana sweetens the batter – no sugar needed.
  • Leftover pancakes can be frozen and reheated in the toaster.
