Mediterranean lentil bowl with roast vegetables and hummus

Beluga lentils with roasted vegetables, rocket, hummus and pumpkin seeds – sunny, filling and great to take with you.

  • Prep15 min
  • Total45 min
  • Servings2
  • Protein per servingapprox. 21 g

Ingredients

serves 2

  • 100 g beluga lentils (dry)
  • 1 courgette
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 red onion
  • 200 g cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 100 g hummus
  • 50 g rocket
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C (top and bottom heat). Cut the courgette, pepper and onion into bite-sized pieces and mix with the tomatoes, olive oil, oregano, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast the vegetables on a lined baking tray for 25 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, rinse the lentils and cook in plenty of water for 20 to 25 minutes until al dente. Drain and season with lemon juice and salt.
  4. Briefly toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry pan.
  5. Arrange the lentils, roast vegetables and rocket in bowls, add a dollop of hummus and sprinkle with pumpkin seeds.

The protein figure is a rough estimate based on average values for the ingredients. To find out how much protein you need per day, use the Protein calculator.

Tips

  • The bowl also tastes good cold and keeps in the fridge for 2 days.
  • For extra satiety, add a portion of spelt.
