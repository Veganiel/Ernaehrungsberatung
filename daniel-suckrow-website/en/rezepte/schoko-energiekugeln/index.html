Chocolate date energy balls with almonds

Little no-bake energy bites: dates, almonds, oats and cocoa – ideal as a sweet snack or dessert.

  • Prep15 min
  • Total45 min
  • Servings8
  • Protein per servingapprox. 4 g

Ingredients

serves 8

  • 150 g Medjool dates, pitted
  • 100 g almonds
  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp desiccated coconut for rolling

Method

  1. Finely grind the almonds and oats in a food processor.
  2. Add the dates, cocoa, chia seeds and salt and blend until a sticky mixture forms. If it is too dry, add 1 to 2 tbsp water.
  3. With damp hands, shape about 16 balls and roll them in the desiccated coconut.
  4. Chill for at least 30 minutes. One serving is 2 balls.

The protein figure is a rough estimate based on average values for the ingredients. To find out how much protein you need per day, use the Protein calculator.

Tips

  • Stored in a container in the fridge, the balls keep for about a week.
  • Dates sweeten naturally and also provide fibre.
