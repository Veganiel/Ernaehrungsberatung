Roast vegetable bowl with pumpkin, chickpeas and tahini dressing

Roasted Hokkaido pumpkin and crispy chickpeas on quinoa and kale, brought together by a creamy tahini and lemon dressing.

  • Prep15 min
  • Total45 min
  • Servings2
  • Protein per servingapprox. 26 g

Ingredients

serves 2

  • 400 g Hokkaido pumpkin
  • 1 tin chickpeas (240 g drained)
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 100 g quinoa
  • 100 g kale or baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • Salt

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C (top and bottom heat). Wash and deseed the pumpkin and cut it into 2 cm cubes, skin on. Cut the onion into wedges.
  2. Rinse the chickpeas and pat them dry. Toss the pumpkin, onion and chickpeas with oil, paprika, cumin and salt and roast on a lined baking tray for 25 to 30 minutes. Turn halfway through.
  3. Rinse the quinoa in a sieve with hot water, bring to the boil with 200 ml water and a pinch of salt and simmer covered for 15 minutes. Remove from the heat and leave to stand for 5 minutes.
  4. Strip the kale from the tough stalks, chop finely and massage with your hands for 1 minute with a pinch of salt and a few drops of lemon juice. This makes it more tender.
  5. For the dressing, stir together the tahini, lemon juice and maple syrup and gradually stir in 3 to 4 tbsp water until creamy. Season with salt.
  6. Arrange the quinoa, kale and roast vegetables in bowls, drizzle with the dressing and sprinkle with pumpkin seeds.

The protein figure is a rough estimate based on average values for the ingredients. To find out how much protein you need per day, use the Protein calculator.

Tips

  • You can eat the skin of Hokkaido pumpkin, so there is no need to peel it.
  • Instead of pumpkin, sweet potato, beetroot or parsnips work well.
