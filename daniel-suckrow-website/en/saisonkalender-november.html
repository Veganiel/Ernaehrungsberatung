What is in season in November

Hardy varieties accompany you through the cold season.

Which fruit and vegetables are in season in November?

In November, the main vegetables in season in Germany are kale, savoy cabbage, salsify, lamb's lettuce, brussels sprouts, squash and leek. For fruit, look out for apples (stored) and pears (stored). Regional seasonal produce is usually fresher, more flavourful and travels shorter distances.

01

Kale pasta with walnuts

  • 25 min
  • Serves 2
  • 4 steps

Ingredients

  • 250 g pasta
  • 200 g kale
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 50 g walnuts
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • lemon juice, chilli flakes

Method

  1. 1

    Bring plenty of salted water to the boil. Wash the kale, strip the leaves from the tough stalks and chop them.

  2. 2

    Cook the pasta according to the packet, adding the kale for the last 3 minutes. Keep a cup of the cooking water, then drain.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, thinly slice the garlic and toast it with the roughly chopped walnuts in 3 tbsp olive oil over a medium heat for 3 minutes until golden.

  4. 4

    Add the pasta and kale to the pan and toss with a little cooking water, nutritional yeast, lemon juice, chilli and salt until glossy.

Tip Kale tastes milder and sweeter after the first frost.

02

Salsify in a light herb sauce

  • 40 min
  • Serves 2
  • 5 steps

Ingredients

  • 500 g salsify
  • 2 tbsp vegan butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 300 ml oat drink
  • parsley, nutmeg, lemon juice
  • boiled potatoes

Method

  1. 1

    Prepare a bowl of water with a splash of lemon juice. Peel the salsify wearing gloves – the milky sap stains your hands – and put them straight into the lemon water.

  2. 2

    Cut the salsify into 4 cm pieces and cook in salted water for 12–15 minutes until just tender. Boil the potatoes at the same time.

  3. 3

    For the sauce, melt the vegan butter in a pan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the oat drink so no lumps form.

  4. 4

    Simmer the sauce for 3 minutes until it thickens and season with salt, nutmeg, lemon juice and chopped parsley.

  5. 5

    Drain the salsify, add it to the sauce and serve with the potatoes.

Tip Salsify tastes nutty and a little like asparagus – which is why it is called “winter asparagus” in German.

03

Lamb's lettuce with pear and pumpkin seeds

  • 10 min
  • Serves 2
  • 4 steps

Ingredients

  • 150 g lamb's lettuce
  • 1 pear
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • 2 tbsp pumpkin seed or rapeseed oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic, 1 tsp maple syrup

Method

  1. 1

    Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes until they start to pop.

  2. 2

    Whisk the oil, balsamic, maple syrup, a pinch of salt and pepper into a dressing.

  3. 3

    Wash the lamb's lettuce thoroughly, pinch off the root ends and spin dry. Quarter and core the pear and cut into thin slices.

  4. 4

    Arrange the lettuce and pear on plates, drizzle with the dressing and sprinkle with the pumpkin seeds.

Tip A few toasted walnuts or smoked tofu cubes turn the salad into a main meal.
