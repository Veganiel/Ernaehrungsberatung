Where can I order my favourite piece?+

The product buttons take you directly to Veganiel’s Shop on Spreadshop. Choose your size and colour and complete your purchase there. Spreadshop handles payment, printing, shipping and order enquiries.

What does organic quality mean here?+

The Make the Connection T-shirt is made of 100 % organic cotton. The End Speciesism and Not the Same but Equal hoodies contain organic cotton. You will find the exact material composition and care instructions on each product page in the Spreadshop.

How do I find the right size?+

Each Spreadshop product page lists available sizes and a size chart. Compare the measurements with a garment that already fits you well.

Where do you deliver and how much is shipping?+

Spreadshop shows the available delivery countries, delivery times and shipping costs. These depend on your delivery address and order.
