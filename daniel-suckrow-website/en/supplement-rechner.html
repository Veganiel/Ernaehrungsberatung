The calculator provides general guidance and not individual medical recommendations. Whether a supplement makes sense depends, among other things, on diet, supply, laboratory values ​​and personal situation. Particularly when it comes to vitamin D/K, minerals and high-dose preparations, intake should be checked individually. If you are ill or taking medication, please seek medical or pharmaceutical advice. More supplements does not automatically mean more health.

Questions and answers

Are these live retailer prices?

Monthly costs use stored illustrative prices. Check your products’ prices and quantities and adjust the available price fields. This tool does not compare live retailer offers.

Does this tell me which supplements I need?

No. Selection follows preset planning rules. Budget, product selection and individual nutritional needs are different things. Planning does not replace checking need, dose or interactions.
