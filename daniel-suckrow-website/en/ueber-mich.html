01

Simple, not perfect.

I look for the levers with the greatest benefit for your health – with little effort and without a complete overhaul.

02

Your life comes first.

I eat a fully plant-based diet myself, but I’m not trying to convince anyone to do the same. Your circumstances, preferences and possibilities set the direction.

03

Small, but lasting.

Small changes that stick achieve more in the long run than big ones that fade after a few weeks.

04

Open to new evidence.

I keep expanding my knowledge and question it critically. When scientific evidence points elsewhere, I would rather change my mind than cling to it.
