Good to know

Planning a vegan diet

A varied plant-based diet can include many nutritious foods. Some nutrients benefit from deliberate planning. In particular, a vegan diet requires reliable vitamin B12 supplementation.

Can the check detect a nutrient deficiency?

The check does not measure total intake, absorption or blood levels. It offers ideas for everyday eating. A medical or nutrition assessment can address your individual needs.

How can I support my nutrient intake in everyday life?

An all-in-one supplement designed for vegan diets and containing vitamin B12 can be an option if its ingredients and amounts suit your needs. A calcium-fortified plant drink can contribute calcium each day. Algae oil containing EPA and DHA is a plant-based source of these omega-3 fatty acids and can be part of a daily routine if needed.

If you suspect a deficiency or have already been diagnosed with one, ask a healthcare professional about assessment and targeted treatment.

Why does vitamin B12 matter on a vegan diet?

A fully plant-based diet requires reliable vitamin B12 supplementation. Seek individual advice on suitable supplementation and monitoring.

Who is the check for?

It’s for adults who eat a vegan diet or want to plan their plant-based diet. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, childhood, adolescence and health conditions can bring different nutritional needs.
